# Ular Ular - Privacy Policy (Short Version)

**Effective Date: February 22, 2026**

## What Information Do We Collect?

**The only information we collect is:**
- Your nickname (chosen by you for gameplay)
- Your game room code
- Your in-game choices and answers

**We do NOT collect:**
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Passwords
- Location data
- Personal identification information
- Payment information

## How Do We Use Your Information?

We use your nickname and game data solely to:
- Enable you to play multiplayer games
- Show your progress to other players in your game room
- Provide educational feedback on quiz answers

## How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

**Very briefly!**
- Your nickname and game data exist ONLY during your active game session
- All data is automatically deleted when the game ends
- We do not store any history or user profiles

## Who Can See Your Information?

- **Other players** in your game room can see your nickname and game actions
- **Nobody else** - we don't share, sell, or use your data for advertising

## Is This App Safe for Children?

Yes! Ular Ular is designed as an educational game for all ages:
- No personal information required
- No chat or messaging features
- No tracking or profiling
- Temporary data only

## Your Rights

- Choose any nickname you want
- Leave the game anytime
- Your data disappears when the game ends
